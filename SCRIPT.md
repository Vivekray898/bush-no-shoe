# Bush Shoe Throw — 2008 (approved script)

## Facts (verified 2026-11-09)
- Source: Wikipedia, "George W. Bush shoe-throwing incident" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Bush_shoe-throwing_incident
- Date: 14 December 2008, Prime Minister's Palace / Republican Palace, Baghdad, Iraq.
- Target: George W. Bush, 43rd President of the United States, at a joint press conference with Iraqi Prime Minister Nouri al-Maliki.
- Iraqi journalist Muntadhar al-Zaidi threw both of his shoes at Bush, shouting "This is a farewell kiss from the Iraqi people, you dog."
- Bush ducked twice, avoiding both shoes. The second shoe collided with a U.S. flag positioned behind Bush. Al-Maliki attempted to catch one shoe.
- Al-Zaidi was grabbed and removed by security. Sentenced to 3 years, reduced; released 15 September 2009 after 9 months.
- Shoes were later destroyed by U.S. and Iraqi security forces.
- Bush's own joke afterwards: "If you want the facts, it's a size 10 shoe that he threw."
- The shoemaker believed to have made the shoes (Ramazan Baydan, Turkey) saw a surge in sales / orders.
- Second shoe shout (reported): "This is for the widows, orphans, and those who died in Iraq."

## Approved narration script (~60 s, ~3.7 words/s, English only, no captions in video)
A journalist threw a shoe at the President of the United States. And he missed. Twice.

December 14, 2008. Baghdad, Iraq. President George W. Bush is at his last press conference there, standing next to Iraq's Prime Minister, Nouri al-Maliki.

A journalist in the front row, Muntadhar al-Zaidi, stands up. He shouts, "This is a farewell kiss from the Iraqi people, you dog!" And he throws his shoe. Bush ducks.

Al-Zaidi throws his other shoe. "This is for the widows and orphans!" Bush ducks again. The second shoe flies past him and hits the American flag behind him.

Luckily, Bush had fast reflexes. Unluckily for the shoe, it only hit a flag.

A thrown shoe flies in a straight line, like a ball in dodgeball. If you drop down fast enough, it just sails over your head.

Al-Zaidi was grabbed by guards and taken away. He was jailed for nine months. The shoes were destroyed.

Bush just joked about it. "If you want the facts," he said, "it's a size 10 shoe." And the man who made that shoe? He suddenly got orders from all over the world.
